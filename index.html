02 — PRODUCTION FIXER

Land in Japan.
Start rolling.

Japan rewards crews who arrive prepared and punishes those who don't. We handle the paperwork, the police, the locations and the people — so your director only has to think about the shot.

Permits & liaison

Road-use permits through local police, filming permission at heritage sites and national parks, ATA Carnet coordination for your kit, and guidance on crew visas.

Scouting & crewing

Locations that match the director's vision and the budget. Bilingual PAs, lighting and art crew, casting and extras — hired locally to cut travel costs.

Logistics & hospitality

Camera and lighting rental, crew vans, on-set catering for halal and vegetarian needs, crew accommodation, and safety and medical cover for water or extreme-weather shoots.

Culture & budget

Script review against local custom and law, realistic local cost estimates, and support applying for Japan's location incentive programmes.

Not flying in? Remote B-roll.

Establishing shots, time-lapses and specific locations captured to your shot list and delivered to your cloud.
